🌾 About Bhoomi Trek

Bhoomi Trek is a smart land acquisition management platform developed to simplify and digitize the land acquisition process. It provides a centralized platform where landowners, citizens and government authorities can access and track land acquisition information.

The platform allows users to search land based on location, view land parcel details, track acquisition progress, check compensation information, submit objections and monitor the final possession process.

🇮🇳 Smart India Hackathon

Bhoomi Trek is designed as a solution for Smart India Hackathon problem statement SIH 26016. The project focuses on using technology to improve the transparency and efficiency of land acquisition management.
